## 1. Summary
The architecture follows the v1.2 scope decisions closely. AI is deferred, with only the reserved seam (overview §6, ADR-0004). Persistence is server-side, with prod-only 7-year retention (§8, ADR-0003/0013). The controlled-data gate runs server-side on free-text writes (§4, ADR-0005). Thresholds live in SSM (§3, `20-data.yaml`). The audit log uses an IAM deny (§7, ADR-0006). I found no material scope creep.

The main gaps are about traceability and precision, not direction:
- **§15 traces only BR-44..47.** No design mapping exists for the other 36 in-scope BRs or for any of the 33 in-scope USs.
- **Three of the four §15 "Consistent" claims are overstated:**
  - BR-46: the IAM policy still allows `PutItem` on AuditLog, which can overwrite an existing event.
  - BR-47: the SSM schema has no re-evaluation or calibration bucket windows.
  - BR-44: the marker-matching rules are undefined, and the marker list differs from the ADR and the diagram.
- **§15 still shows C-1 as an open clarification.** Vishwas decided it and BRD v1.2.2 records the decision.
- **Some rules are designed vaguely, and the design quietly fills in answers.** These are segregation of duties (who counts as "independent"), workflow transition rules, SCAR origin, and the Approval Roles "Remove", which conflicts with an IAM deny.

## 2. Verdict
**Approve with changes.** No blocker. Fix the major findings (BA-01..BA-07) in the overview/ADRs before development starts on records-api, the audit log and configuration. Vishwas needs to decide the items in §5.

## 5. Assumptions needing Vishwas's decision
1. **SoD independence (BA-07, OQ-03):** what makes an approver "independent" for Use as is / Repair? Is it name ≠ reviewerName, or name ≠ any actor on the record? Is a name comparison acceptable until auth exists?
2. **Simulate second reviewer (BA-07, BR-33):** keep or remove in the build.
3. **Controlled-data markers (BA-04, O-04):** the final list (EAR? CFR citations?) and the matching rule (case-insensitive whole word?). Also: which fields are screened, and whether IDs/names are exempt (BA-05).
4. **Threshold values and bucket structure (BA-06, O-04):** per-module due-soon windows, dashboard/KPI bucket boundaries, risk weights, and band cut-offs and labels.
5. **Workflow transitions (BA-08, OQ-06/07):** backward moves, who closes, whether a disposition is required before Closed, and the SQ re-evaluation cycle.
6. **SCAR origin (BA-13, OQ-23):** NCMR-only or a standalone create.
7. **Supplier inputs (BA-12):** are on-time % and open SCAR count entered by hand or derived? What are the deterministic flag and next-step rules?
8. **Retention start and disposal (BA-14):** does the 7-year clock start at creation or closure? Is data disposed of after that?
9. **Production ID format and seed/sample data per env (BA-15, OQ-10).**
10. **Dev/stage reset procedure owner (BA-03):** who may run it and how. (The principle is already decided by C-1.)
